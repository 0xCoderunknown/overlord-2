## 🛡️ 3. Armor & Ballistic Protection

Armor directly reduces incoming enemy damage per attack via Damage Reduction (DR):
$$\text{Net Damage Taken} = \max(0, \text{Enemy Roll} - \text{Armor DR Roll})$$
